Beep Boop

A robot on the roof keeps blinking and beeping at us. The intern was supposed to write it down. He didn't. Press play and decode it yourself (turn your sound on).

Short flash = dot. Long flash = dash. A longer gap means a new letter, and an even longer one means a new word.
